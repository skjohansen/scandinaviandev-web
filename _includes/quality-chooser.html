The four important quality characteristics of software

What do you want to achieve?

The four following quality characteristics share one goal: better software. Pick the one you are chasing to see how we help you get there — better software is more secure software, and more software is easier to sell and cheaper to maintain.

Maintainability Code the next engineer — and the AI — can read is code they can both secure. Security Protection of information and data — with resilience as the goal. Performance Efficiency Software that performs well under load — and stays in control when it does. Reliability Systems that keep doing what they should, even under stress and failure.
