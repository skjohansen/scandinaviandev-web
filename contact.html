About & Contact

A Swedish company, founded in 2019

Scandinavian dev exists to train a generation of Scandinavian organisations not just to survive in the digital age, but to thrive in it.

The company was founded in 2019 with a deep focus on software development and the conviction that it deserves to be treated as an engineering discipline — with the standards, rigour and accountability that word carries.

Security is a quality attribute. Writing secure code has a positive effect on every other quality parameter, and the reverse is just as true: poor quality is poor security. That is why the work starts inside the engineering team rather than beside it.

Poor quality is poor security.
